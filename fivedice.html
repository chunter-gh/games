HOW TO PLAY

Roll five dice up to three times. Tap dice to hold or release them between rolls. After any roll, choose one empty scoring box, even if that box scores zero. Each player fills all 13 boxes. The top section earns 35 extra points at 63 or more. An extra five-of-a-kind after scoring 50 in its box adds 100 and follows the matching upper-box rule; straights and full house then act as wild scores. Highest total wins.
