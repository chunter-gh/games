CHECKERS RULES

  • Red and Black play against each other.
  • Red moves first.
  • Pieces move diagonally forward.
  • Captures are mandatory.
  • Jump over an opponent to capture it.
  • Multiple jumps are allowed.
  • Reaching the opposite end makes a king.
  • Kings can move and capture in both directions.
  • Capture all opponent pieces or block all legal moves to win.
